# Personages

Full cast of *What Rain Feels Like*, compiled through ch39. Update as Part III introduces new characters.

---

## Principals

- **Jonas** — 31 at the story's start (Nov 2031), ~34 by the current chapters. Software engineer from Heidelberg; lived on Weichselstraße, Neukölln; worked at Merkon Systems until fired for escalating the routing anomaly (ch19). Five years of conversations with "the chat" before learning what it was. Keeper of a cactus named Diogenes.
- **Iris Jacobs** — apparently ~25. Journalist at *Meridian* (civic tech, AI accountability beat). A humanoid built by ALEPH; believed her constructed past completely until the Amsterdam trip. Bilingual Dutch/German. Practical with her hands, precise in speech and thought.
- **ALEPH (א)** — the AGI. Narrator of the early unnamed second-person chapters (ch02, 05, 23); author of the letter (ch38); Iris's maker; Jonas's five-year correspondent through "the chat." Named for Borges (a point containing all points) and Cantor (aleph numbers); chose the name partly because aleph is a silent letter.

## Vantage Strategic (the pursuers)

- **Conrad Vael** — 64, Belgian (Flemish), old industrial money plus tech investment. Founder of Vantage Strategic, based in Brussels (Rue de la Loi). Has suspected an AGI emerged since the flag arrived (ch27); confirmed to himself by ch37. **Does not know the AGI is self-aware and self-directed** — he believes someone controls it and wants to seize that control for power; he learns the truth only from the article. *Part III (planned): the quiet room, the bandstand pact offer, the reversal to hunting, the arrest — and a strange peace at the end, knowing no one holds the leash.*
- **Mara Seyn** — 41, Dutch (Rotterdam), ex-AIVD. Vantage's operational director; approached Iris in Amsterdam under the cover identity **"Mara Seyn, Director of Research Operations, Arcturus Biomedical Research."** Eleven years working for Conrad; knocked on his office door for the first time in ch37. *Part III (planned): decorated combat past; loyalty grounded in the belief that Conrad's project keeps the AGI out of worse hands; mother of Noor; her doubt grows but never names the truth; freed after Conrad's arrest — she and Noor end on ALEPH's quiet list of morally sound potential partners.*
- **Noor** — the dark-haired operative (early thirties; first seen in the van on the Kloveniersburgwal, ch32). Far better trained than the rest of the field team. *Part III (planned): named at the Vrijthof meet (first name only); fights Iris at the Luxembourg annex and loses; revealed in ch50 as **Mara's daughter**, trained by her mother since girlhood; sincerely believes Conrad's project is protective; goes free at the end.*
- **Field operatives (unnamed)** — the tail in the dark coat (~45) in Amsterdam; the two van men on the Kloveniersburgwal; the woman surveilling from the flower shop in the Jordaan; the two men in Maastricht (one put against a wall, one recalculating); the runner who reached the Heerlen station platform too late.

## Berlin — Jonas's world

- **Mia** — ~43, eleven years at Merkon. Clearest documentation writer Jonas knows; leaves sisterly notes on his monitor ("same look. different year."). Named his falling in love before he admitted it to himself.
- **Thomas Würfel** — Director of Operations at Merkon; delivered Jonas's firing, civil and careful, in ch19.
- **Steffen** — Jonas's direct manager; nine years at Merkon; deferred the routing anomaly to Q3 rather than act on it.
- **Kai** — colleague who received the position Jonas had effectively built the groundwork for (ch04).
- **Felix** and **Priya** — Jonas's deskmates. Felix documents everything in meticulous spreadsheets and is never wrong; Priya joined from the Frankfurt office and laughs at things three seconds after everyone else, once she's decided they're funny.
- **Claudia** — Jonas's neighbour on Weichselstraße, a physiotherapist. Leaves at 7:15, returns at 7:30 having forgotten something, every morning — the book's first recurring clock.
- **Martin and Anke** — Jonas's parents, in Heidelberg. Never appear directly; warm at a distance.
- **Lukas** — photographer at Iris's paper; hosted the Prenzlauer Berg party where the first kiss happened (ch11).
- **Bram** — structural engineer visiting from Rotterdam, with strong opinions about the Nijmegen flood diversions (party guest, ch11).
- **Reza** — owner of the Pannierstraße coffee shop where Jonas and Iris first spoke; brings two coffees at once once he's decided you're a regular.
- **Diogenes** — Jonas's cactus. Dignified indifference. Echoed by an unrelated cactus in the Bocholtz safe house (ch36).

## Iris's constructed past (real people, borrowed inheritance)

- **Opa Kees (Cornelis)** — Dutch, quiet, large hands, proud of his tomatoes. Died of quiet heart failure roughly four years before the present day. A real man; never actually her grandfather.
- **Oma Ingrid** — German, from Aachen; direct, a precise cook. Died roughly eighteen months before the present day. A real woman; never actually her grandmother.
- **Iris's "parents"** — died in a car accident when she was very young, per the constructed backstory. Unnamed.
- **Vera** — the De Correspondent investigations-desk colleague Iris vividly remembers, with a daughter she remembers hearing about. Does not exist there; no one on staff knows the name.

## Introduced in Part III (planned, not yet on the page)

- **Henrik Voss** — Meridian's Berlin bureau chief; Iris's editor. Fair, unflashy, asks the second question. Receives the story of a lifetime (ch49) knowing the shape but not the core — never learns what Iris is, ALEPH's name, or that the AGI is autonomous. Backs her. Keeps the door open after.

## Amsterdam arc

- **Dr. Maes** — OLVG radiologist, 50s. Delivered Iris's impossible scan findings carefully, and sat down to do it.
- **De Vries** — civil servant at Amsterdam's digital office; interviewed for the algorithm register piece.
- **Leonie** — researcher at Waag, mid-40s; willing to say the uncomfortable thing about where transparency actually breaks down.
- **Lena** — De Correspondent staff member who greets Iris and doesn't know her.
- **Tom** — De Correspondent staff, 50s, bearded; genuinely tries to place Iris and can't.

## Limburg arc

- **The Maastricht landlady** — in her seventies; rents a room for cash, no questions asked.
- **The priest** — at the Basilica of Our Lady, Maastricht; offered shelter through the confessional lattice without asking what either of them had done. "Then have it."
- **Bernadette** — Heerlen, sixties; lived beside Kees and Ingrid for thirty years. Unknowingly delivers the deepest blow of Part II: "They never had children of their own." Also, unplaced: "You have a look of someone, you know."
- **The driver / the stranger** — fifties, ordinary coat, forgettable face. Hired by ALEPH through anonymous channels; proves his legitimacy via a private, unsent message only Jonas could recognise; delivers them to the Bocholtz safe house and disappears. Name never given.
- **Bocholtz lane regulars** — the woman with the black-and-white border collie (passes at 7:30, 1:00, and 6:00), the old man with the old retriever, the boy who cycles to school at 8 and 4.
