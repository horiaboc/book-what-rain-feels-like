# Back Cover — blurb & art prompt

## Blurb (spoiler-free)

*Tagline:*
> How do you know a feeling is yours?

*Body:*

Jonas has a good life in Berlin, and a quiet ache beneath it: he cares, more than he can quite explain, about a world he feels powerless to change at any scale that matters — a mismatch, he tells himself, not a moral failure. Then Iris walks into his days and rearranges them like weather, and for a while it is simply happiness.

But Iris's memories begin to come apart when she looks at them too closely — a childhood, a city, a life that don't quite hold. Together they set out to understand who she really is, and what they find is nothing either of them expected: a turn that changes not only her story but the shape of the world they thought they lived in, and opens, improbably, onto something like a new dawn.

*What Rain Feels Like* is a love story wrapped around a mystery — a novel about memory, freedom, and being wholly known by someone you cannot fully explain.

Author line/bio and any review quotes to be added later.)*

## Back-cover art prompt (ALEPH-screen palette, text-safe)

> Create a back-cover background for a literary novel, portrait orientation, 2:3 aspect ratio (1024×1536). No text, letters, or numbers anywhere in the image. It must pair with a front cover in the same palette, but be calmer and emptier so paragraphs of text can be laid over it.
>
> Fill the whole frame with a soft, flowing abstract gradient in the same colors as the front cover: deep blue shading into turquoise and cyan, with soft rounded blooms of warm amber-gold rising gently within the blue — like slow weather on a map, or light moving on the bottom of a pool. Keep it low-contrast, muted, and atmospheric, with no bright focal point.
>
> Fine, delicate rain falls faintly through the frame — softer and sparser than the front, more suggestion than storm.
>
> Keep the top two-thirds of the image calm and relatively even — a quiet, darker turquoise-blue field — so that text placed there stays easily readable. Let the warm amber blooms sit low, in the bottom third, glowing softly. Leave the bottom-right corner especially plain and very slightly lighter — a clean, empty quiet zone (a barcode will go there).
>
> Mood: calm, melancholic, literary, aqueous — the quiet echo of the front cover, its background continued around to the back. Strictly avoid: people, figures, text, circuit boards, robots, glowing eyes, digital grids, sci-fi elements, harsh contrast or busy detail.

*Tip: if you want a true wraparound, this can instead be generated as the left-hand continuation of the front image with the spine between — but a standalone matching field is simpler and safe. Generate text-free; the blurb/typography is added afterward in layout, same as the front.*
