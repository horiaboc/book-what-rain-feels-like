Always verify real-world location references before writing or finalizing chapters. Street names, venue addresses, U-Bahn/S-Bahn lines and stops, canal names, neighborhoods — all must be accurate.

**Why:** Found one error in ch08 (Kulturforum placed on Kurfürstenstraße — it's near Potsdamer Platz/Matthäikirchplatz). User asked to keep locations accurate going forward.

**How to apply:** Before writing any chapter with a specific address or venue, confirm it. Fictional cafes, unnamed bars etc. are fine. Named real venues need correct placement. When in doubt, use a neighborhood reference rather than a specific street.
